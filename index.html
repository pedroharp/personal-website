01 — About

Engineer by training, strategist by practice.

I work where engineering, product and finance meet. My teams build agentic AI, generative AI and Salesforce platforms, and much of my job is turning ambiguous problems from marketing, sales, finance and operations into scoped, sized and funded product work.

I started as a software engineer in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, building .NET and SharePoint solutions and leading a Scrum team at CI&T. I joined Dell in 2011, moved to the United States, and worked my way from project management through Salesforce consulting to leading engineering. That path taught me to speak both the language of the codebase and the language of the P&L.

I care about building teams that ship: mentoring engineers every cycle, holding a high bar for delivery and security standards, and staying hands-on with the technology. In 2025 I completed UT Austin's Post Graduate Program in AI & Machine Learning at the McCombs School of Business, covering machine learning, neural networks, NLP with generative AI, computer vision and model deployment.
