Case study 02 · Dell Technologies

Turning every sales chat into coaching

200+ signals per conversation, in US pilot

In pilot with 12 US chat reps since September 2026. Impact figures below are projections.

Role
Product owner and executive driver
Focus
  • ML
  • LLMs
  • Analytics

The problem

Dell's consumer inside-sales team handles about a million customer conversations a year across hundreds of reps worldwide. When one rep finds an approach that closes more sales, nobody else sees it. Coaches can read a handful of chats a week, and raw transcripts aren't insight.

What we built

A coaching platform with three views:

  • Reps see how each conversation went and what to try next.
  • Coaches get a prioritized coaching queue and a library of best practices.
  • Leaders get team rankings and a drill-down into why a number moved.

Under it, an LLM pipeline extracts more than 200 features from each chat: objections, open loops, buyer readiness and more. A purchase-propensity model, trained on 37,000 historical chats, scores each conversation against what we would have expected. A sale within 30 days counts as a win, which separates the rep's effect from the customer's intent.

The data comes from six systems: contact-center transcripts, web analytics, sales KPIs, the internal LLM platform, app hosting and single sign-on.

A design choice worth noting

We did not point the chat assistant at raw transcripts. It answers from computed tables and model outputs, so every answer traces back to something measured.

Results so far

  • Pilot live with 12 US chat reps. In the first review of 17 chats, reps rated most insights useful and flagged 7 improvements
  • Projected to save each coach about 2.5 hours a day on chat review (projection)
  • Next: a leader-facing "ask the data" assistant, then voice and more countries

My role

Product owner and executive driver. I built the business case, secured a VP sponsor, and took the AI use case through Dell's privacy review. That review led Dell to update its public privacy notice to cover AI analysis of chat conversations. When single sign-on onboarding was quoted at 60 days, I pushed an interim login so the pilot didn't wait.
